# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

- **A picture mode for the window:** `--shot FILE.png` (built with `--features gui`) opens the window at the size of `--size WxH` (320 to 8192, 1180x760 by default), with `--theme forge|dark|light`, `--lang CODE` and the file of `--open FILE`, waits `--wait N` frames (0 to 3600, 6 by default), saves what it drew as a PNG file and closes by itself. An existing file is never replaced unless `--replace` is given, the window keeps no settings and reads no settings file in this mode, the exit code is 0 when the file was written, 1 when it was not (also when the picture does not come back within 600 frames) and 2 for wrong arguments, and a build without the window says so. The arguments are read by plain code with its tests.

## [0.0.4] - Vector cutting and engraving from SVG and DXF, the window with a table of layers and the Windows icon

- **Ready-made programs for every system:** the `Release binaries` workflow, started by hand by the owner from main, builds the window version for Windows (64 and 32 bits), Linux and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), packs each with the licence, the README, the icon and the examples, writes the SHA-256 of every package, `SHA256SUMS` and a build manifest, attests their provenance, checks the result as a stranger would and, only when the dry run is switched off and the owner approves, prepares a draft release; the README has a Downloads section that tells how the signed packages are checked.
- **Vector drawings are read:** `svg` reads a safe subset of SVG (`path` with `M L H V C S Q T A Z` absolute and relative, `rect` with round corners, `circle`, `ellipse`, `line`, `polyline`, `polygon`, `transform`, groups, the layers of Inkscape, stroke and fill colours, the units `mm cm in pt pc px` with a settable `dpi`) and `dxf` reads `LINE`, `LWPOLYLINE` and `POLYLINE` with bulges, `ARC`, `CIRCLE` and `SPLINE` (through its fit points, or from its control points, knots and weights) with the unit of the file. Scripts, images, `use`, text, gradients and styles with selectors are never run or fetched and are skipped with a note; a declared entity is an error. Curves become straight pieces that stay within a settable tolerance of the true curve. The size of the file, the depth, the elements, the shapes, the points and the pieces of a curve are limited by settings.
- **Cutting and engraving by layer:** the shapes are grouped by colour (SVG) or layer (DXF); each group is cut or engraved with its own power, speed and passes. Cutting joins loose lines whose ends meet, cuts the contours that lie inside another one first and keeps the travel with the laser off short (the nearest next contour and then a limited 2-opt improvement that never breaks the inside-first rule), moves a closed contour half the width of the beam to the right side (outside a piece and inside a hole, or always one side) with round corners and a check of every edge of the result, leaves tabs uncut on the outline of a piece, and moves the focus on at each pass. Engraving fills closed shapes with lines at an angle and spacing (the even-odd rule, one way and then the other, with overscan) and follows open ones. A contour that is too small or too narrow for the beam is left out with a note instead of being cut wrong.
- **The settings are in a file:** the limits of the readers, how a file is read, the order of the cuts, the machine (full power, overscan, speed of the travel, origin, focus, bed) and the settings of each layer or colour are keys of a plain text file that is checked line by line (an unknown key or a value out of range is an error that names its line); nothing tuned is in the code. `docs/vector-settings.md` has every key with its default and its range, and `examples/plate.svg`, `examples/plate.dxf` and `examples/plate.cfg` show them at work.
- **A G-code that is checked before it is given:** the program of a drawing keeps the form of the one of a picture (`G21`, `G90`, `M4 S0`, `S0` while it travels, `M5` and `M2` at the end) and every line is checked: only the words the program writes, `S` never above full power, no laser on during a rapid move or a move of the focus, no cut without a speed, no move outside of the bed (when its size is given), and a power that rounds to `S0` is refused. A program that fails is not written. The file is never written over the drawing or the settings it is made from.
- **Commands `cut`, `vector` and `layers`:** `cut DRAWING OUT.nc` follows the contours, `vector DRAWING OUT.nc` fills the closed shapes with lines, and `layers DRAWING` lists the layers with what would be done with each; they take `--config FILE` and the options `--power`, `--speed`, `--passes`, `--focus-step`, `--kerf`, `--kerf-side`, `--tabs`, `--tab-width`, `--angle`, `--spacing`, `--smax`, `--overscan`, `--origin`, `--bed`, `--dpi` and `--tolerance` over the settings of every layer, and `--dry-run` works the job out without writing.
- **The window opens drawings:** an SVG or a DXF is opened from the list of files or dropped on the window and the program switches to cutting; the path of the laser is drawn with the cut in one colour and the travel dim, the table of layers (mode, power, speed, passes, step of the focus, width of the beam and its side, tabs, hatch angle and spacing) is edited in the window, a settings file can be loaded, the notes of the reading and of the plan are listed, the time is estimated and the G-code is exported with the same header as the one of a picture. The words are in the eight languages.
- **A window:** built with `--features gui`, `phoenix-laser` with no arguments (or with one picture, or `gui [IMAGE]`) opens a window in the look of the other programs of the suite (amber on charcoal, the icon of the program). A PNG or PGM picture is opened from the list of files of the window or dropped on it; the passes of the laser are drawn as the head makes them (burning in a colour that follows the power, travel and overscan thin), with zoom (wheel) and pan (drag); the width, the size of a dot, the dithering and its threshold, the feed, the power, the full power value, the overscan (typed or worked out from the acceleration) and the origin can be edited, a calibration grid of power against feed can be made, and the G-code is exported with the same header as the command line. The command line works as before, and a build without the feature is only a command line.
- **The window speaks the eight languages:** English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Chinese and Portuguese of Portugal, chosen from the system and changeable in the window.
- **The logic of the window needs no screen:** `window` (picture, settings, engraving, passes, export, status), `viewport` (zoom and pan), `browse` (the list of files) and `lang` (the words) are plain code with their own tests; `gui` only draws them.
- **The Rust version needed is 1.88,** the one the window needs.

- **Renamed:** The project is now called EH3D-PHOENIX-LASER (it was EH3D-LASER-CAM); it joins the PHOENIX family of programs.

## [0.0.3] - Audit fixes, the new reading limits, tests and the application icon

- **The application icon and the logos of the program:** `images/brand/` holds the icon (SVG, PNG sizes, `.ico` and `.icns`), the horizontal and vertical logos in a light and a dark version and the social preview image.
- **Shared checks updated:** the validator also compares the badge and the Version row of the README with the manifest, fails when one of its checks cannot run, and the CHANGELOG step of the workflow reads the base branch from the environment.
- **Every G-code file opens with a standard header:** comment lines with the generator and its version, the units (mm, G21), the picture it was made from with its SHA-256 and the tools (the laser and its power); the calibration grid says it has no source. The library functions keep returning the program alone, so the header belongs to the files the command line writes.
- **One way to write a number, and no `NaN` or `inf` in a file:** the engraving and the calibration grid write every number with the same function (`gcode_out::number`: three decimals at most, no `-0`), and a value that is not finite is an error. The SHA-256 used by the header is written in the program itself (no new dependency) and checked against the known test vectors.
- **The reader of pictures comes from EH3D-COMMON (`eh3d-image`):** the program no longer keeps its own copy of the PGM and PNG reader; it takes it by path from the sibling repository, which must be cloned beside this one. `eh3d_*::image` keeps working as before. The manifest names EH3D-COMMON in `depends_on` and the CI checks it out.
- **More tests:** the G-code read back into the dots it was made from, the limits of the head and of every value, the calibration grid square by square, and the command line with its errors and file names in any script.
- **The workflows are hardened:** every action is pinned by its commit with its version in a comment, and the checkouts keep no credentials.
- **More tests of the limits:** every value of the parameters at its edges, what the engraving reports, and the shape of the lines at the ends.

## [0.0.2] - Dry run for engraving

- **Strict command line:** an option that is written wrong (`--powr`) or a word too many is an error that names it; before, it was ignored and the output was made with the default values.
- **A dot size that makes no sense is refused before anything is reserved:** `--dot-mm 0` or a tiny one, or an image that would be larger than the limit, gives an error instead of an allocation failure or an overflow.
- **`engrave --dry-run`:** works the engraving out and counts it (dots, lines, runs, burnt length, time), without writing the output file (which may then be left out).
- **One shared helper for the command-line arguments** (`src/cli_args.rs`: flag, switch, num and a strict finish), the same file in every small utility of the suite; the copies this program had behaved the same, so nothing changes for a person.
- **Tests that run what the README and the scripts promise:** the sample of `run.sh`, `run.bat` and the README is the same command, and it is run on the files of `examples/`.
- **The banner of the README** shows the role and the technologies of this program (it showed the text of the shared library).

## [0.0.1] - An image engraved by a laser: dithering, raster G-code with dynamic power and a calibration grid

- **The image:** PNG (grey, colour, transparent, 8 or 16 bits) and PGM, with the size limited; `resize` averages the pixels.
- **The dots:** threshold, Floyd-Steinberg and Atkinson, checked to keep the darkness of a grey.
- **The laser:** `M4` with `S0` while it travels, alternating directions, an overscan fixed or worked out from the acceleration, and the top of the image as the highest Y.
- **The calibration grid:** a square for every pair of a power and a feed.
- **The command line** `eh3d-lasercam` with `engrave`, `calibrate` and `info`, and a sample gradient.
- **18 tests** run on a computer, with damaged input.
